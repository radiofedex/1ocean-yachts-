About 1 Ocean Yachts Group

25 years of personalised yachting excellence.

Our Story

25 Years of Yachting Excellence

1 Ocean Yachts Group was established with a commitment to delivering personalised, high-quality service in a market often driven by volume. Based in Miami, Florida, we operate as an exclusive dealer for world-renowned brands and serve as a trusted partner for yacht sales, charters, management, new construction and advisory services for ultra-luxury assets.

What began as exclusive representation of a single brand has grown into a multi-brand powerhouse representing Palmer Johnson and G-Tender, two of the most prestigious names in luxury yachting.

We are active at the world's most prestigious yacht shows — Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Palm Beach, Monaco and Cannes — maintaining a global presence that reflects our international clientele.

Our Founder

Jose Arana Muñoz De Cote

Founder & CEO

With over 25 years of experience in luxury yachting, Jose has guided ultra-high-net-worth clients through every aspect of yacht ownership and charter. His career began in 1997 at Ferretti of America and has since included senior roles at Koch Newton & Partners, Merrill-Stevens Yachts, and Fraser Yachts — one of the world's leading superyacht brokerages.

Prior to yachting, Jose developed a strong commercial background in marketing and business strategy at Microsoft, Ericsson, and Bell South. Fluent in English, Spanish, and Italian, he serves a global clientele with complete discretion and expertise.

The Brand

Iconic Design, Performance & Luxury

Palmer Johnson has operated for nearly a century, evolving from a wooden boat builder to a contemporary luxury yacht manufacturer known for groundbreaking design. Their vessels represent a statement of timeless elegance, built for those who seek the extraordinary in both design and performance.

1 Ocean Yachts Group is the exclusive dealer for Palmer Johnson in the United States, offering our clients access to the full range of Palmer Johnson models — including the revolutionary SuperSport series — with full after-sales support, warranty and dockage at our Miami facility.
