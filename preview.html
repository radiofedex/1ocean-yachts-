Our Founder

Jose Arana Muñoz De Cote

Founder & CEO · Yacht Broker · Brand Representative

Jose's career in luxury yachting began in 1997 with Ferretti of America. Over the following decades he held key positions at Koch Newton & Partners, Merrill-Stevens Yachts, and Fraser Yachts — one of the world's largest superyacht brokerage firms — building an unrivalled depth of industry knowledge and a network spanning every major yachting market.

Throughout his career Jose has represented the world's most prestigious yacht builders. A defining chapter has been the strategic growth of 1 Ocean Yachts Group's dealership division: his ability to align with top-tier manufacturers and drive market penetration has positioned the firm as a dominant force across sales, charter and brand representation — including the exclusive dealership for Palmer Johnson and G-Tender.

Fluent in English, Spanish and Italian, Jose brings a uniquely global perspective to every client relationship — serving ultra-high-net-worth individuals with absolute discretion, from first inquiry to long-term ownership.

Palmer Johnson

Nearly a century of iconic design, relentless performance and uncompromising luxury — represented exclusively in the United States by 1 Ocean Yachts Group.

The Brand

Iconic Design, Performance & Luxury

Palmer Johnson has operated for nearly a century, evolving from a wooden boat builder to a contemporary luxury yacht manufacturer known for groundbreaking design. Their vessels represent a statement of timeless elegance, built for those who seek the extraordinary in both design and performance.

1 Ocean Yachts Group is the exclusive dealer for Palmer Johnson in the United States, offering our clients access to the full range of Palmer Johnson models — including the revolutionary SuperSport series — with full after-sales support, warranty and dockage at our Miami facility.

  • Customisable luxury interiors
